- Clipping: long sources are hub library `video` assets with `role = source`, uploaded via TUS; analysed only by AssemblyAI (resumable via `transcript_id` + `analyze-asset {resume}`) and `gemini-frames` on browser-made contact sheets (`meta.frames`); `find-clips` reads the whole transcript in one pass. Clips are ranges (`projects.plan.clip`) over the original; before rendering, "Prepare clip" losslessly cuts just those ranges in the browser (`src/features/clipping/cut-clip.ts`, range requests, keyframe-aligned) into a project `video` asset with role `clip-cut` and `meta.ranges`, and `render-video` redirects source <Video>/<Audio> to it with mapped trims; `clipSection` in `_shared/editor-context.ts` feeds the editor. Why: no 20 MB limit, full context; Lambda seeking deep into multi-GB remote files timed out even at 15 minutes.
- Clip framing: faces are tracked in the browser with MediaPipe FaceLandmarker (`src/features/clipping/track.ts`) over the clip ranges only, saved as `projects.plan.clip.camera`; the editor must render source footage through the pasted `ClipCamera` helper (`_shared/clip-camera.ts`). Why: LLM-written crop maths and Gemini snapshot boxes framed faces offscreen.
- Clip sources get a no-re-encode sound copy (`meta.audio_path`, made in the browser by `src/features/clipping/extract-audio.ts`, served by `asset-url?part=audio`); `render-video` swaps in the current ClipCamera, rewrites OffthreadVideo→Video, mutes source videos, refuses sources without a sound copy, and uses a 120s delayRender timeout. Why: Lambda downloads whole unmuted/Offthread media; multi-GB sources overflow its 2 GB disk.
