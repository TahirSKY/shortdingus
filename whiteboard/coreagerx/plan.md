# CoreAgeRX — whiteboard pitch

**Project** `coreagerx` · hub `whiteboard` · 1920x1080, 30fps, **234.0s = 7020 frames**
**Voiceover** 0.176 → 233.055s (233s read, 1s of air on the end card).

## The story
CoreAgeRX is not one product, it is an **online healthcare and wellness platform** covering
GLP-1 weight management, skincare and longevity. The pitch is that a platform like that sells
something people need to *understand* before they buy, so education is the product's best
salesperson — and the whiteboard video is the cheapest way to make that education.

That gives the film a spine the Gala cut did not have: **three things, again and again.**
Three services appear in the opening, return when the topic turns to what we'd explain, and
return again in the packages. The motif does the remembering for the viewer.

## Craft bar (raised from the Gala cut)
Gala had several drawings that were wide thin bands (1843x239) sitting in a sea of white.
This time every scene is drawn to **fill its 16:9 frame, roughly 85% of the height**, so the
art sits in the band like a poster instead of a strip. Composition notes are per scene.

- Thin wobbly ink, flat fills only, no shading, no gradients, no paper texture.
- Palette: ink `#1A1A1A` · sky `#A9D9F0` · sage `#B7C9A8` · amber `#F2CE72` · coral `#EFA08C`.
- Exactly one of each named object — the prompt says it twice.
- **No text, no letters, no numerals, no logos.** Prices and durations are typeset in code.
- One idea per drawing, literal and tactile.

## Hard rules
1. **A drawing is never cut mid-draw.** Clip speeds up to finish ≥0.3s before the cut, then
   the identical still holds. `rate` rounds **up**, never below 1.
2. **Horizontal renders need `format: "horizontal"`** on `whiteboard-animate`.
3. **Clips are not full-bleed.** Engine paints the PNG at `scale 0.84074`, so the clip canvas
   is drawn at `s / 0.84074` and the still at `s`; the swap cannot jump.
4. **No numerals in generated images.**
5. Art plays in an 888px band with a 192px caption strip under it.

## Build

**Voiceover** `eef9c8cd-4be3-4a89-a702-232c7244d4c2` — "Generated Audio November 04, 2026 - 2_25PM.wav",
`audio/wav`, 233.36s. Sits at frame 0; every scene start is measured off its word timings.

**Composition settings** 1920x1080 · 30fps · 7020 frames.

**Geometry** art band 888px, caption strip 192px, `V_PAD 22`. Art is `888 x 1578.67`, centred.
Clips are the engine's 84.074% inset, so the video element is scaled by `1/0.84074` about the same centre —
that is what lets a scene jump from clip to freeze without a visible pop.

**No stills in the build.** Every scene is a clip. Where a scene is longer than the clip's 7s, the clip is
frozen at its final frame (`<Freeze>`) rather than swapped for a PNG — same pixels, zero geometry risk.
Scenes affected: s05 (2 frames), s13 (23), s14 (16), s32 (38).

**Rates** `max(1, 6/(len-0.35))`. Only s01 (still) and s44 (2.6s) run fast at 2.67x. Everything else is
1.0-1.6x. No rate below 1, so no scene ever crawls its pencil.

**Sound** pen noise is baked into each clip at `volume 0.07` — present, never muted, never loud.
