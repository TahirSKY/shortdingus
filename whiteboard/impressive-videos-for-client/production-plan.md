# Impressive videos for client: production plan

## Concept
A self-demonstrating whiteboard sales video: open on feed fatigue, make the viewer's own attention the proof, show the founder's motivation, then demonstrate the offer and end on a direct comment/DM CTA. Retain the supplied voiceover and its candid founder story unless the client requests a rewrite.

## Source
- Project: `impressive-videos-for-client` in the whiteboard Hub.
- Narration: `9fbbd7ac-373f-4f8b-a768-e5f1236c9b0e`, 52.96 seconds. Manifest provides 186 timestamped words.
- Intended format: 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, about 54 seconds with a brief end hold.
