How The Agent Uses It

Persistent analysis — available to the app

When you upload: Gemini returns timecoded beats — scene descriptions with timestamps, emotion labels, dialogue mentions, visual events.

When you prompt me: the app can retrieve the saved analysis by video name or analysis ID. If you say "make a hook at the part where he talks about the garage", I find the beat near 0:12 with that dialogue and build the edit around that timestamp.

Saved to: private cloud video storage plus a structured analysis record.

Agent Integration — How I Use Your Beats

When working on your video, before I suggest hooks or edits, the app retrieves its saved analysis record. If a record exists with your video name, I reference the timecodes in my response — "At 0:08 when he gestures toward camera is a great hook — let's zoom in there." This is how you get agentic video understanding instead of blind editing.
